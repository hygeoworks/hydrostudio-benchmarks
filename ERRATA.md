# Published example errata and target adjustments

In 11 of the 69 cases the published value is not used directly as the pass/fail target: the source contains a misprint, a formula or chart reading that cannot be reproduced from its own inputs, or rounded intermediate values, or (S-01, S-13) the reference comes from programs that differ in method from an exact solution.
The published value is kept unchanged in `reference`; the target used for the check is in `target` (`expected` in `cases.json`) together with its basis. Differences and tolerances in `cases.csv` are measured against `target`.
